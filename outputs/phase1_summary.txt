5. KEY RELATIONSHIPS (EDA FINDINGS)
================================================================================

Finding 1: DISTANCE is the strongest predictor
- Correlation with rate: 0.908
- Rate-per-mile DECREASES with distance (economies of scale)
  • 0-300 miles:   $2.74/mile
  • 1500-2000 mi:  $2.04/mile
  • 2000+ miles:   $1.94/mile

Finding 2: EQUIPMENT TYPE matters
- Rate per mile: Reefer ($2.38) > Flatbed ($2.29) > Dry Van ($2.12)
- Mean rates: Reefer ($2,554) > Flatbed ($2,445) > Dry Van ($2,272)

Finding 3: WEIGHT has minimal direct impact
- Correlation with rate: 0.035
- Effect captured indirectly through distance/equipment

Finding 4: MARKET INDEX & QUOTE SIGNAL
- Market index correlation: 0.034 (weak in training, but 14% drop in validation!)
- Quote signal correlation: -0.040 (surprisingly negative, but may be useful)

Finding 5: TEMPORAL PATTERNS
- Monthly variation exists (rates peak in June: $2,497)
- Weekday vs weekend: minimal difference ($2,382 vs $2,354)
- Some seasonality visible across months

Finding 6: LANE-LEVEL VARIATION
- 4,014 unique lanes in training
- High-volume lanes (30+ loads) show consistent pricing
- Low-volume lanes have high variability (volatile pricing)

Finding 7: GEOGRAPHIC PATTERNS
- West Coast origins (San Francisco, Fresno, Phoenix) = highest rates
- Central/Southeast origins (Memphis, Nashville, Cincinnati) = lowest rates
- Distance dominates, but origin/destination add predictive value

Finding 8: OUTLIERS
- 1% of rates > $5,973 (99th percentile)
- Mostly long-haul (mean distance 2,484 miles)
- Reefer and Flatbed overrepresented in outliers

6. DECISIONS MADE
================================================================================

Decision 1: Cleaning Strategy
- Fix negative weights (absolute value)
- Impute missing values (median)
- Remove extreme outliers in training only (< $0.40 or > $15/mile RPM)
- Keep all validation rows (corrected, not removed)

Decision 2: Validation Strategy (→ Phase 2)
- PRIMARY: Time-based holdout (train on Jan-Sep, validate on Oct)
- SECONDARY: Expanding-window time-series CV for robustness
- COMPARISON: Random K-fold to demonstrate leakage/optimism

Decision 3: Feature Engineering Focus (→ Phase 3)
- Calendar features (month, day of week, holidays, seasonality)
- Lane-level aggregations (with strict train-only leakage protection)
- Distance/weight buckets and interactions
- Handling unseen lanes (fallback to region/distance-based estimates)

Decision 4: Modeling Strategy (→ Phase 4)
- Baseline: Naive rate-per-mile × distance
- Linear: Regularized regression
- Main: Gradient boosting (LightGBM/XGBoost/HistGB)
- Consider log(rate) vs rate as target
- Tune on time-based CV only
