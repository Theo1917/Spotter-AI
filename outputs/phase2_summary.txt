================================================================================
VALIDATION APPROACH JUSTIFICATION
================================================================================

WHY TIME-BASED VALIDATION IS MANDATORY:

1. The Task is Future Forecasting
   - Training data: January - October 2025
   - Real validation: November - December 2025
   - We must predict rates 1-2 months into the future
   
2. Random Splits Cause Temporal Leakage
   - Random K-fold: training includes dates AFTER validation dates
   - All 5 random folds show 304 days of temporal overlap
   - Model learns from "the future" → artificially optimistic metrics
   - Does NOT reflect real-world performance

3. Lane-Level Information Leakage
   - Same lanes appear in both train and validation with random splits
   - Model memorizes lane-specific patterns from future time periods
   - Overestimates ability to handle unseen lanes (17.5% in real validation)

CONCLUSION: Time-based validation is the ONLY valid approach for this task.

================================================================================
IMPLEMENTED VALIDATION STRATEGY
================================================================================

1. PRIMARY HOLDOUT (for final model selection)
   --------------------------------------------
   Training:   January 1 - September 30, 2025 (9 months, 43,129 rows)
   Validation: October 1 - October 31, 2025 (1 month, 4,851 rows)
   
   Rationale:
   - October is the most recent month in train_test.csv
   - Mimics the 1-month forecast gap to real validation (Nov-Dec)
   - Provides realistic performance estimates
   
   Validation Set Characteristics:
   - 0 unseen pickup cities (all seen in training)
   - 0 unseen delivery cities (all seen in training)
   - 6 unseen lanes (0.2% - much lower than real 17.5%)
   - Note: Real validation will be harder due to more unseen lanes

2. TIME-SERIES CROSS-VALIDATION (for robustness checks)
   ------------------------------------------------------
   3 folds with expanding training window:
   
   Fold 1: Train Jan-Jul (33,702 rows) → Validate Aug (4,758 rows)
   Fold 2: Train Jan-Aug (38,460 rows) → Validate Sep (4,669 rows)
   Fold 3: Train Jan-Sep (43,129 rows) → Validate Oct (4,851 rows)
   
   Rationale:
   - Tests model stability across different time periods
   - Expanding window matches how we'd train in production
   - Used for hyperparameter tuning and ablation studies

3. RANDOM K-FOLD (comparison only - NOT for training)
   ---------------------------------------------------
   5 folds with random shuffling:
   - Each fold: 38,384 train / 9,596 validation
   - ALL folds have 304 days of temporal overlap ⚠️
   - Training includes future dates relative to validation
   
   Purpose:
   - Demonstrate why random splits are inappropriate
   - Compare metrics to show optimistic bias
   - Include in report to justify time-based approach

================================================================================
EVALUATION METRICS
================================================================================

Primary Metric: MAE (Mean Absolute Error)
- Direct interpretation: average dollar error per prediction
- Robust to outliers
- Business-relevant: "On average, we're off by $X"
- Used for model selection and comparison

Secondary Metrics:
- RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error): Penalizes large errors more
- MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error): Scale-independent, %
- R² (Coefficient of Determination): Variance explained (0-1)

Segment Analysis (for each metric):
- By distance bucket: 0-300, 300-600, 600-1000, 1000-1500, 1500-2000, 2000+ miles
- By equipment type: Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed
- By month: Seasonal patterns and trends

Purpose: Identify where the model performs well/poorly

================================================================================
FINAL MODEL TRAINING PROCEDURE
================================================================================

After model selection and validation:

1. Retrain the chosen model on ALL of train_test.csv (Jan-Oct, 47,980 rows)
2. Apply to validation.csv (Nov-Dec, 12,000 rows) for submission
3. Generate December chart predictions (31 fixed-lane forecasts)

This ensures we use maximum available data for the final predictions.
