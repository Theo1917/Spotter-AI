================================================================================
DECEMBER CHART ISSUE & EXPLANATION
================================================================================

Why is the December chart flat?

The December chart shows minimal variation ($0.27 std over 31 days) because:

1. Fixed Lane: Same pickup, delivery, distance every day
   → Lane mean rate dominates (learned from training data)

2. Fixed Physical Characteristics: Same equipment, weight
   → Equipment and weight features are constant

3. Weak Calendar Effects: Model learned minimal day-of-week/seasonality
   → Most variation in freight rates comes from lane and distance, not dates
   → Holidays (Christmas) do affect rates, but the model captured limited signal

4. Model Behavior: Rate-per-mile model multiplies by distance
   → Since distance is fixed at 360 miles, only RPM varies
   → RPM prediction is stable for this specific lane

Real-World Expectation:
In reality, rates for a lane might vary 5-15% across December due to:
- Holiday demand spikes (pre-Christmas rush)
- Capacity constraints (fewer trucks available around holidays)
- Weather impacts
- Spot market volatility

Our model learned these patterns weakly because:
- Training data may have limited holiday examples for this specific lane
- Temporal features were outweighed by structural features (distance, lane)
- The task focuses on cross-sectional prediction (many lanes) vs. time series

Possible Improvements (if more time):
- Add stronger holiday indicators (e.g., days-until-Christmas)
- Model volatility separately (predict mean and variance)
- Use time series models for temporal components
- Ensemble with a specialized calendar model
